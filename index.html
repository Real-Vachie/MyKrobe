Explore the World of MyKrobe

Extruder buildings are an environmental feature that release particles into the world. It acts as source point for resources within the world to enable the player to plan and react.

Protein particles give Krobe strength. These particles do not move quite as fast or far as some of the livelier varieties.

Caffeine will increase Krobe’s speed, eventually leading them to be able to phase through walls. It is definitely a more challenging particle to contain.

Grow buildings. Each Organelle Building encourages certain play styles by influencing the world’s production.
(Placeholder animation)

Buildings will be influenced by their growth conditions allowing for personalized and repeated game-play. Eg; increased production or increased particle potency

Advanced buildings such as Effectors can attract or repel particles without Krobe needing to being present.

Bosses lurk in the world, waiting to be woken up and defeated!

Features

A living world harbors Krobe and it will respond to them. Specialize in farming certain particles by growing organelle buildings. These buildings cause the world to offer up different resources.

  • The world offers a variety of physical particles that exhibit different behaviors and change Krobe. When eaten by Krobe these particles add attributes like health, speed, size, strength, and modifies capabilities.
  • Eat enough of some particles, or find special particles to gain Ultimate Specializations.
    • Ultimate Specializations (WIP):
      • Speed offers the ability to phase through walls and obstacles.
      • Strength allows you to cull enemies below 15% health - even bosses!
      • Health allows you to become nearly invulnerable.
      • Become quantum and manipulate particles from a distance.

Manage Krobe:

  • Krobe will need to be kept healthy to stay alive. Manage expelled wastes properly or face the hostile pooparticles.
  • Refine Particles by physically managing the particles to obtain pure concentrated particles. Careful not to mix in different particles in this process!
  • Manage particles using organelle building that attract or repel certain particles.
  • A hazardous world. From static to dynamic, from living creatures to inanimate obstacles and dangerous puzzles- there are dangerous things in here!
  • Bosses will be found during your exploration, enabling Krobe to discover new abilities by consuming the bosses particles when defeated.
  • Raise critters to help take care of things around the base!
  • Special Event Particles - Some particles spawn randomly around the world and when consumed cause Krobe to hallucinate into a mini-game. Conquer the mini-game to obtain new powers and abilities for Krobe!
  • Cosmetics - from Hats and clothes to base decorations
